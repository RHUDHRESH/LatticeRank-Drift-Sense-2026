Research findings

Why registration is difficult

The reference spans only a few lattice periods, while imaging changes the signal before matching begins.

01

Periodic aliases

Repeated cells create several convincing correlation peaks. The runner-up matters almost as much as the winner.

02

Unstable intensity

Dose, blur, detector noise, drift, shear, and polarity shifts make raw brightness an unreliable identity cue.

03

Unknown pose

Phase 2 spans 8–12× scale and roughly ±5° rotation. Pose must be estimated before precise translation.

04

CAD domain gap

Phase 3 starts with stacked polygons. Layer contrast may be positive, negative, faint, or absent in the SEM image.

Pipeline

Coarse evidence to subpixel pose

Edges preserve geometry across photometric changes. Independent proposals prevent a single representation from deciding every case.

1

Normalize & gate

Robust contrast and gradient energy reject unusable inputs.

2

Extract edges

Gaussian smoothing and normalized Scharr magnitude retain structural boundaries.

3

Propose pose

SIFT/RANSAC and directional FFT evidence propose legal rotation and scale.

4

Correlate

Warped references search the full field with ZNCC; multiple remote peaks survive.

5

Refine

Bounded Powell optimization updates x, y, theta, and scale continuously.

6

Decide

Peak quality, ambiguity, and pose support produce confidence and abstention.

Known weaknesses

What the stress tests exposed

These diagnostic suites are intentionally harsher than the measured 25-pair runs and are reported separately.

Phase 2

Candidate recall

Periodic structure can displace the correct spatial peak even when scale and rotation are already correct.

Phase 2

Acceptance calibration

Four stress positives landed within 0.31–2.03 px of truth internally but were rejected.

Phase 3

Negative rejection

All eight independently generated hard negatives were accepted in the stress suite.

Phase 3

Sampling order bias

Capped vector traversal favors early polygons; bottom placements underperform top placements.
